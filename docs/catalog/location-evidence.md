# Catalogue Location Evidence

Generated by `scripts/build_school_location_evidence.py`; evidence snapshot 2026-10-15.

Every one of the 4,140 catalogue names has one record. `mapped` entries are the existing city map and retain their source metadata. Unmapped entries remain null until an exact institution/address source verifies them; names are never used as a city inference.

| Status | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| `campus_or_import_unverified` | 149 | Campus or import variant needs exact address |
| `graduate_relation_unverified` | 38 | Graduate relation/location needs explicit evidence |
| `historical_or_alias_unverified` | 7 | Historical/renamed entry needs dated evidence |
| `legacy_unresolved` | 37 | No exact source found in frozen snapshot |
| `mapped` | 3797 | Existing evidence-backed city mapping |
| `official_no_city` | 112 | MOE adult record has no city field |

The report intentionally distinguishes catalogue participation from city coverage: adult institutions with a null Ministry city and unresolved WakeUp variants participate in the audit but are not assigned a guessed city.

A mapped institution has at least one recorded city. This does not imply every campus is covered or that campus coordinates are known. Graduate-office records identify the published office or training-unit city only. Per-record coverage notes and input hashes are retained in the JSON inventory.

Actual search queries, result URLs, probe outcomes and source snapshot hashes are recorded in school_location_research.json for unresolved entries. Search results and HTTP success are research traces, not verified address evidence.
