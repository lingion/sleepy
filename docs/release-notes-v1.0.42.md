# Sleepy v1.0.42

### Scale the grid and week view
The grid now has a 70%–130% zoom slider and a separate corner-radius slider. Week View has its own zoom and can split days into two columns, either evenly or by course count. Empty days can be hidden.

### Every widget has a compact variant
Today, TwoDay, WeekList, WeekGrid, and WeekView now each offer a small layout with a preview in the widget picker.

### Choose the launch page
A setting now chooses whether the schedule opens on Week View or Grid View. Manual switching during use is not saved.

### Settings names describe their scope
Appearance & display became Appearance & theme, and display controls moved into Home display settings. Labels now explain when a setting affects the schedule, widgets, or both.

# Sleepy v1.0.42

### 网格和周视图支持缩放
网格新增 70%–130% 缩放滑杆和独立圆角滑杆。周视图拥有自己的缩放，可以按天对半分栏或按课程数平衡分栏，也可以隐藏无课日。

### 五种小组件都有紧凑变体
Today、TwoDay、WeekList、WeekGrid 和 WeekView 都新增小尺寸布局，添加小组件时可以先看预览。

### 可以选择启动页面
现在可以设置打开课表时进入周视图或网格视图。使用中手动切换不会被保存。

### 设置名称会说明影响范围
“外观与显示”改为“外观与主题”，显示选项移到主页显示设置。设置文案现在会说明它影响课表、小组件，还是两者。

- versionCode：`43`
